How It Works

How A Car Comes In

A fixed sequence. No shortcuts between wash and final inspection.

01

Inspection & Wash

Full paint inspection under raking light, decontamination wash, clay bar if needed.

02

Correction

Machine polishing staged to the paint's actual condition. Never a one-size pass.

03

Protection / Tint

Ceramic coating or precision window tint applied in a controlled bay.

04

Final Inspection

Panel-by-panel sign-off before the car leaves: edges, gloss, and finish checked in daylight.
